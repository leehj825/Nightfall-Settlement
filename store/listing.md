**Full description** (max 4000, 2060 used):

Hold the night. Build the realm.

Nightfall Settlement is a village defense saga. Raise a camp into a walled city, train your soldiers, and lead them against the raiders who come every night - then take the war to their castles.

BUILD AND GROW
- Grow your settlement through five stages: Camp, Wooden Town, Stone Fortress, Walled City and the Seat of Power.
- Plan houses, farms, a blacksmith, barracks, archery range, market, stables, towers and three rings of walls with gatehouses.
- Keep your people fed, housed and happy. More homes mean more citizens, more workers and more soldiers.
- Watch the seasons turn, the weather change and the night close in.

DEFEND AT NIGHT
- Fight beside your soldiers in real-time third-person combat: sword, great sword, spear and bow.
- Block, dash, jump and unleash your ultimate. Ride a horse and leap over fences and walls.
- Survive Blood Moon raids, siege engines, brutes, archers and bosses. Towers, walls and gates all matter.

COMMAND YOUR WARBANDS
- Organize soldiers into warbands with their own banners, names and orders: Forward, Hold, Fall back, Charge.
- Place each band on the battlefield, choose a formation - block, line, wedge or shield wall - and mark the enemy you want dead.
- Every soldier grows on their own. Fighters gain strength and speed from battle, and citizens master their trade.
- Rally the host under one banner and lead cavalry charges.

MARCH AND BESIEGE
- Send expeditions to ruined towers, bandit camps, hill clan strongholds and iron legion forts.
- Prepare before the battle begins, then break the gate with rams, raise ladders and fight through boiling oil to take the castle.
- Explore a campaign map of rival realms, garrison what you win and march to the final siege of the Iron Throne.

MADE FOR PLAY ANYWHERE
- Touch controls built for phones and tablets, with a hide-and-show option.
- Full gamepad and keyboard support.
- Original music, a day and night cycle and a low-poly look that runs smoothly.
- Single player. Supported by a small banner ad.
