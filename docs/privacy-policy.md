# Privacy Policy — Nightfall Settlement

Nightfall Settlement does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or network features.

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device (local storage) and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The app requests the vibration permission solely for haptic feedback, which can be turned off in Settings.
